# Local SEO & Google Business Profile

For any local-business build (restaurant, clinic, salon, law firm…). Maps ranking has direct revenue causality and the work is templated.

## GBP 100% checklist
- Claim + verify; fill EVERY field: name, primary+secondary category, address, phone, website, hours
- 20+ photos: exterior, interior, products/food, team
- Services/products with descriptions and prices
- Weekly posts (events/offers); booking link configured
- Every review answered within 24h

## NAP consistency (the silent ranking killer)
Name/Address/Phone IDENTICAL across GBP, site footer, socials, every directory — punctuation included ("Street 100" vs "100 Street" counts as inconsistent). Bulk push via Yext/BrightLocal-class tools; audit annually.

## Citations
NAP mentions on other sites; target 50+ quality citations in year 1. Prioritize country-level directories + industry-specific platforms (restaurant aggregators, medical directories, legal directories per market).

## Reviews = #1 local ranking factor
- Target 100+ Google reviews, ≥4.5 average
- Acquisition: post-service email with one-click review link + QR at point of sale
- NEVER fake reviews — detection → penalty → possible listing deletion
- Respond to all, positive and negative, within 24h

## Multi-location pattern (3+ locations)
One landing page per location: `/city` slug, unique 200+ word intro, location photos, embedded map, local testimonials, NAP, LocalBusiness (or subtype) schema; all linked from a /locations hub.

## Hyper-local content
Neighbourhood-intent pages ("<category> in <district>"), local-event posts, placement in "best of <city>" aggregators, backlinks from city blogs/chamber/local press.

## Generation pattern
For business type + city → emit nested checklist: GBP setup, NAP checks, top-30 citations for the category/market, review-acquisition email template, JSON-LD schema template, tracking setup.
